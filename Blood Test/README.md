## Blood Test

***Winter, 1982. An isolated research station in Antarctica.***

![View on an Antarctic station from a laboratory](https://ucarecdn.com/ab5baee6-9056-4f45-ae7c-b47c9d076eae/)

Something is hiding among the crew.

An alien organism has infiltrated the station. It can perfectly imitate any human, copying their appearance, voice, and behavior. No one knows how many crew members have already been replaced.

After hours of arguing, the station's doctor finally comes up with a way to identify the impostors: an experimental blood comparison test.

The test takes blood samples from two crew members and reveals whether they belong to the **same species** or **different species**. Unfortunately, it cannot tell which sample is human and which belongs to the alien. Note that there are only two species: humans and the alien.

> There is one piece of good news: the crew member with ID **1** has already been independently verified as human.

Your dataset begins with two integers, `N` and `M`, representing the number of crew members and the number of blood tests.

Each of the following `M` lines contains two crew member IDs and a test result:

- `a b SAME` — both crew members belong to the same species.
- `a b DIFFERENT` — one is human and the other is an alien.

All test results are accurate, and there is enough information to determine the identity of every crew member.

**Your answer should be the total number of aliens among the crew, submitted as a single integer.**

*Remember: anyone could be one of them.*
